# Quick guide to playing

## Getting started

1. Put on your headphones: the left one on your left ear.
2. Open `EntornoLabJugar.exe`.
3. Choose your alias in "Who is playing?". If it is your first time, choose "I'm new: type my alias". A nickname is better than your full name.
4. In the menu, do the "Tutorial" first.
5. Then choose "Choose environment", the place and the activity.

In the menus: Up Arrow and Down Arrow to move, Enter to choose, Escape to go back, and F1 to find out what an option does.

## The most important keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Up Arrow and Down Arrow | Move forward and back |
| Left Arrow and Right Arrow | Turn |
| Enter or Space | Open a door, pick up a reward, call the elevator |
| C | Where am I |
| W, A, S and D | Look ahead, to the left, behind and to the right |
| Q | What is around |
| B | Sound cane |
| O | What do I have to do |
| P | Hint |
| M | Confirm in an exam: "I've arrived" or "this is my answer" |
| F2 | Repeat the last thing |
| Home | Go back to the starting point |
| F1 | Help |
| Escape | Pause |

With a controller: the left stick walks and turns, A or Cross interacts, X or Square tells you where you are, Y or Triangle looks ahead, the right trigger is the cane, and the Menu or Options button pauses.

## Tips

- Bumping into things is not bad: it tells you where things are.
- If you get lost, ask where you are (C) or ask for a hint (P).
- Before playing, go through "Learn the sounds" in the main menu.
- If your instructor gives you a code, choose "Connect to my instructor".
- Take breaks and don't turn the volume up too high.
- This is a game for learning a place. In a real street, you always cross based on what you hear there and with your instructor.

## Version for children

1. Put on your headphones.
2. Open the game.
3. Choose your game name.
4. Play the "Tutorial" first.
5. Up Arrow: you walk. Arrows to the sides: you turn.
6. Enter: you open doors and pick up rewards.
7. C: the game tells you where you are.
8. P: the game gives you a hint.
9. Escape: you stop the game to take a rest.
10. If you bump into something, that's fine: that's how you find out what is there.
